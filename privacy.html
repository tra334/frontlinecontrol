Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

An individual based in the UK runs Frontline Control (“the Game”, frontlinecontrol.co.uk) and is the controller of your personal data under UK GDPR. Contact: admin@frontlinecontrol.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), plus the dates you signed up and last signed in.
  • Player profile: your display name (3–20 characters).
  • Game data: your dispatch progress, including missions and jobs completed, stations and units, earnings, rank, daily and rank bonuses, and settings.
  • Rooms and chat: rooms you create or join, who is in them, when you were last online, invites, and the messages you send.
  • Forum (optional): if you sign in to our community forum with your game account, the forum receives the account details it needs to sign you in and stores your posts and messages.

Frontline Control is a free hobby project. We don’t monetise it or earn any income from it. We don’t take payments, show ads, run analytics or send marketing emails. The Game doesn’t use your device’s location. Our hosting providers may process IP addresses technically to run the Game; we don’t use them to identify or track you.

What other players can see

  • Everyone: if you reach the leaderboard (top 50), your display name, earnings and jobs completed. Other players can also search for your username to invite you to a room. Forum posts are public.
  • Players in your room: your display name, last-seen time, stations and units, and your chat messages.
  • Nobody: your email address.

Please don’t use your real name or share personal details in your display name, chat or forum posts.

Who can access your data

We (the owner) and our moderators can access player data through our admin tools, only as needed to run the Game, moderate chat and the forum, and deal with cheating and abuse.

Services we use

  • Supabase (EU) stores accounts and cloud saves.
  • A UK-based host runs the game server and our community forum (Discourse).
  • Maps: tiles and place lookups come from OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap, and map code loads from public code networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg).
  • Radio: in-game radio audio streams from third-party providers.

Your browser contacts the map, code and radio providers directly, so they may see your IP address. We don’t sell your data.

Our lawful bases

We use your data to provide the Game you signed up for (contract), and to keep it secure, fair and moderated (legitimate interests).

How long we keep it

Game data is kept until you delete your account (Menu > Account > Delete account). That permanently removes your account, cloud save, room and chat data, and leaderboard entry. Forum posts and your forum account are handled separately: email us to have them deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or give you a copy of your data, or object to how we use it. Email admin@frontlinecontrol.co.uk; we’ll reply within one month.

Local storage, no cookies

The Game saves your login, settings and a local copy of your game in your browser’s storage. It’s needed for the Game to work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies in the Game. The debug log stays on your device unless you copy and send it to us yourself.

Children

The Game is for players aged 13 and over. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it. Players under 18 should keep chat and forum posts free of personal details.

Security

We use encrypted connections and hashed passwords. Please choose a strong, unique password.

Changes

We’ll post any update here with a new date.
